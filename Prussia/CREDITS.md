# Prussia art credits

All final game assets are cropped, resized, recolored where noted, and encoded as DDS files for this Civilization V mod. No generative artwork is used.

## Royal Prussian eagle

- Source: [Royal Eagle of Prussia.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Royal_Eagle_of_Prussia.svg)
- Creator: Wikimedia Commons user BrazilianInBattleofVerdun
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Adaptation: rasterized, inset inside the Prussian blue and white icon frame, and recolored from black to Prussian blue while preserving white, gold, and red heraldic details. Its alpha silhouette supplies the civilization and unit-flag emblem.

## Frederick II portrait

## Firaxis base-game assets

Civilization V assets are reused in-game for Germany's soundtrack, European architecture, the normal Great General 3D model and era variations, Heroic Epic building presentation, promotion art, and standard gameplay audio. These remain property of their respective rights holders and are not redistributed as standalone source files.

Ethnic Units v31 is an optional compatibility target. The mod references its `_GERMANY` family only when that mod is enabled and its German art definitions are present; no Ethnic Units asset is bundled or required.
